02 / The quick version

First, the big picture.

Three ideas make every rotation easier to read.

01
↻

Rotation is your starting order

Six players start in numbered spots. When your team wins the serve back, everyone rotates one spot clockwise. You do not rotate after every point.

02
⇢

Then you move to your job

Your rotation spot and your playing role are different. Once your rule set allows movement, head to the space where your role helps the team most.

03
◎

Three touches, one plan

Usually: pass or dig, set, then hit. On their serve, receive first. On your serve, get ready to defend the return.

WORDS YOU'LL HEAR

Volleyball, in plain English.

Pass / receive Control a serve or easy ball toward the setter.

Dig Keep an opponent’s attack from hitting the floor.

Set Put up a ball a teammate can hit.

Hit / attack Send the ball toward the other team to score.

Block A front-row player stops or slows a hit at the net.

Sideout Win the rally while receiving; now you rotate and serve.

Stack Start close to your playing job while keeping the required rotation order.

04 / Play by the right rules

The small print, made useful.

Checked against official sources on 13 October 2026.

!

One important difference

Under FIVB 2025–28, the serving team can stand freely at the serve. The receiving team must keep its rotation relationships until serve contact. Under USA Volleyball 2025–27, both teams keep those relationships until serve contact. A 2026 FIVB trial used at specified competitions lets receivers move when the server begins the serving motion, after being correctly positioned at the whistle.

That trial is not automatically your local league’s rule. Choose the rulebook your competition uses.

01

Front row / back row

Spots 4, 3, 2 are front row. Spots 5, 6, 1 are back row. Your rotation row still controls who may block or attack above the net from in front of the attack line, even after you move.

02

Libero and substitutions

The libero plays in the back row and cannot complete a block or an attack with the ball fully above the net. FIVB forbids libero serving; USA Volleyball permits one service position per set.

03

2026 rule updates

USA Volleyball now permits a double contact on the second team touch when a teammate plays it next; a catch or throw is still a fault. Serving teammates must keep their hands at or below head height. FIVB's 2026 movement trial applies only at selected events.

04

What “attack” means here

The app uses “our serve” and “their serve” to avoid confusion. In volleyball, the receiving team often gets the first attack; the serving team prepares to defend it.
